# `getMarginAccount`, `getAccountEquity` and `getOpenOrdersOnchain` read at a block behind the receipt the SDK has just returned for the caller's own order
Severity: normal
Area: SDK

## Steps to reproduce
1. `npm install` (pins `@somnia-chain/markets-sdk` 0.32.1).
2. Use a testnet key with STT for gas and about 50 USDso of perps margin (the saved runs started with 49.6861, 49.6561 or 49.6171 USDso unlocked; the orders are the SOL minimum size, 0.1). Put it in `PROBE_PRIVATE_KEY`.
3. Margin reads: `PROBE_PRIVATE_KEY=0x… CYCLES=10 node repro/016-margin-reads-pinned-behind-own-fill.mjs`. Each cycle: an IOC market buy of 0.1 SOL (fills at the touch; the SDK returns the mined receipt), then at once `client.getMarginAccount` and `client.getAccountEquity`; then the closing IOC sell and the same reads. Control: `client.getPerpPosition` (no pin: one `readContract` without `blockNumber`, `dist/perp/state.js` lines 252-265). Each cycle costs the touch spread (the 10 cycles of run 1 lowered the account from 49.6861 to 49.6561 USDso, the `funds` field of the two margin evidence files); every cycle closes its own position.
4. Order list: `PROBE_PRIVATE_KEY=0x… CYCLES=20 node repro/016-open-orders-onchain-pinned-behind-own-receipt.mjs`. Each cycle: `trader.placePerpOrder` for a resting bid 20% below the mark, then at once `client.getOpenOrdersOnchain([pool], owner)`; then `trader.cancelOrder` and the same read. Control: `client.getOwnOpenOrdersOnchain(pool, owner)` at the same moment. Second control (run 3 on; runs 1 and 2 are from the version without it): the same `getOpenOrdersOnchain` read with `{ blockNumber: receipt.blockNumber }`, right after the pinned read. The order never fills (about 40 small transactions).

## Expected
The account reads are chain-tier reads: `dist/perp/margin.d.ts` lines 52-54 (`getMarginAccount`): "The SDK samples the chain head once and pins every component read to that block. All returned fields therefore describe one chain state, even if the head advances while the fan-out is in flight." `getAccountEquity` reads through `getMarginEquity`, documented as `equity` and `lockedCollateral` "pinned to one block" (lines 57-60). These lines promise one consistent block, not a block that includes the caller's last write. But the SDK docs searched (README, `docs/`, the JSDoc of these reads) do not say that the head they sample can be below a receipt the SDK has already returned, and `getMarginAccount` gives the caller no way to choose the block, so a caller who checks their margin right after their own fill expects to see that fill, or a doc note that it may be missing.
`getOpenOrdersOnchain` (JSDoc, `dist/orders.d.ts` lines 653-665): "Use when you must list your own resting orders and cannot depend on the indexer ... This read answers from the chain itself". Its `blockNumber` option is described as "Read at this exact block instead of pinning the current head" (line 647). The how-tos `docs/how-to/detect-fills.md` line 61 ("Read your own writes at chain head") and `docs/how-to/handle-errors.md` lines 127-128 tell the caller to read their open orders at chain head to learn whether a write happened ("send again only when that state shows the write did not happen"). They name `getOwnOpenOrdersOnchain`, which does show the write (the control below); `getOpenOrdersOnchain` is its full-rows sibling in the same read-tier table (`docs/reference/read-tiers.md` line 227).

## Actual
The pin is `getBlockNumber()` (`eth_blockNumber`): `dist/perp/margin.js` line 33 (`getMarginAccount`), line 66 (`getMarginEquity`, which `getAccountEquity` calls at `dist/accountEquity.js` line 29), `dist/orders.js` line 574 (`getOpenOrdersOnchain`: `opts?.blockNumber ?? (await pc.getBlockNumber())`). Right after the SDK has returned the receipt of the caller's own transaction, `eth_blockNumber` often returned a block below that receipt's block (by 1 to 6 blocks; over all 200 pinned reads in the saved runs the gap was 0 to 6), so the read describes the state before that transaction. Why the node's `eth_blockNumber` trails a receipt it has already served is not determined by this report; the unpinned reads in the same moments (controls below) already see the write.

Margin reads (`evidence/016-margin-run1.json`, `evidence/016-margin-run2.json`; 10 open + close cycles each, 0.1 SOL IOC fills, 100,000,000 base units each way in all 40 orders):
- After the opening fill, `getMarginAccount` was read at a block behind the fill's receipt in 4 of 10 (run 1) and 10 of 10 (run 2) cycles, and showed no active perp pool (the pre-fill account) in 4 of 10 and 9 of 10. `getAccountEquity`'s `asOfBlock` was behind the receipt in 4 and 10 of 10 (the evidence records its block, not the equity value). The control `getPerpPosition` showed the 0.1 SOL position in 10 of 10 and 10 of 10.
- After the closing fill, `getMarginAccount` was behind the receipt in 8 and 3 of 10 and still listed the pool as active in 7 and 2 of 10; `getAccountEquity` was behind in 8 and 3 of 10. The control showed flat in 10 of 10 and 10 of 10.

Order list (`evidence/016-run1.json`, `evidence/016-run2.json`, `evidence/016-run3.json`; 20 place + cancel cycles each):
- After `placePerpOrder`, the pinned read was behind the receipt in 8 of 20 (run 1), 20 of 20 (run 2) and 14 of 20 (run 3) cycles and the placed order was missing from `getOpenOrdersOnchain` in all of those.
- After `cancelOrder`, the pinned read was behind the receipt in 13, 2 and 6 of 20, and listed the already-cancelled order in 12, 2 and 6 of 20.
- Control in the same moments, all three runs: `getOwnOpenOrdersOnchain` (no pin: it passes `blockNumber` only when given one, `dist/orders.js` line 490, so the `eth_call` is at latest) agreed with the receipt in 60 of 60 placements and 60 of 60 cancellations.
- Run 3, same `getOpenOrdersOnchain` with `{ blockNumber: receipt.blockNumber }`: showed the placed order in 20 of 20 and the cancelled order gone in 20 of 20, no read error. The read is right once it is pinned at the caller's receipt; the default pin is what misses the write.

Every `getMarginAccount` and `getOpenOrdersOnchain` read that was behind showed a state from before the caller's write (for `getAccountEquity` only the block was recorded). Where a "behind" count is one higher than the matching "shows the old state" count (margin run 1 `i` = 2 and run 2 `i` = 1 after the close, margin run 2 `i` = 9 after the open, order run 1 `i` = 14 after the cancel), the pinned block was also below the receipt of the transaction before (the opening fill, the previous cycle's close, the placement), so the read showed the state before that one.

The results carry `asOfBlock` / `blockNumber`, so a caller who compares them with their own receipt's block could see the lag; no SDK doc for these reads advises comparing it with your own receipt's block (`grep -i asOfBlock` over `docs/` only finds `PERPS.md` lines 338-347, which use `asOfBlock` to pin follow-up reads). The caller cannot pin `getMarginAccount` at all: `SomniaMarketsClient.getMarginAccount(marginBank, account)` (`dist/somniaMarketsClient.d.ts` line 3212) takes no block option. `getAccountEquity` (`blockNumber`, `dist/accountEquity.d.ts` line 23) and `getOpenOrdersOnchain` (`blockNumber`) do, so there the caller can pass the receipt's block (run 3, tested on `getOpenOrdersOnchain` only: right in 40 of 40), but nothing tells them they must. Code that checks margin or equity straight after its own fill could read the account as it was before the trade, and code that rebuilds its quote list from `getOpenOrdersOnchain` could see a quote as missing or a cancelled one as resting. The documented `getOwnOpenOrdersOnchain` was not affected in these runs.
The same `getBlockNumber()` pin is in `getBookTopsOnchain` (`dist/orders.js` line 249) and `drainOpenOrdersOnchain` (line 719); this report did not test them.

## Suggested fix
Document on these reads that the head they pin can be below the caller's last receipt, and that passing the receipt's block (`blockNumber`; run 3, `getOpenOrdersOnchain`: right in 40 of 40) or the unpinned `getOwnOpenOrdersOnchain` / `getPerpPosition` is the read-your-writes path; add a `blockNumber` option to `getMarginAccount` like the one `getAccountEquity` and `getOpenOrdersOnchain` already have. Optionally, have these helpers wait until `eth_blockNumber` reaches the writer's last receipt block before pinning (a read at a just-mined block can fail on this node now and then, see report 015, so keep a retry with it).
