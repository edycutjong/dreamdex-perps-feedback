## https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/perps-testnet/margin-and-liquidations (Paper USDso)
Paper USDso

The 1,000 USDso that Perps Arena gives you is a credit voucher. It counts as margin like any other USDso, with a few rules while any of it remains in the account:

It can't be withdrawn. Only USDso above the voucher balance can leave the Perps account.
Only some markets. You can open or increase positions only in the markets marked Yes under Paper USDso in the market table. The six newest markets are not on that list.
Your leverage setting can't be changed while it remains.
Reducing and closing are never blocked, in any market.
No builder fees are charged on the account.

You can add your own USDso alongside it; both sit in one balance. A voucher can be taken back 14 days after it was granted, but only while the account has no positions and no resting orders: it never forces a position closed.

## https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/perps-testnet/funding (page body)
Funding
A perp has no expiry date, so funding payments keep its price close to the asset's index price instead. Funding is paid directly between longs and shorts: the protocol takes no cut.
Who pays whom
Perp above the index (positive funding): longs pay shorts.
Perp below the index (negative funding): shorts pay longs.
Paying the side that trades against the gap is what pulls the perp back toward the index.
When it is paid
Rate window	8 hours
Settlement	Every hour
The funding rate is quoted per 8-hour window, and each hourly settlement applies an eighth of it:
Hourly payment = position size × index price × funding rate / 8
For example, a 1 BTC long with the index at 100,000 USDso and a funding rate of 0.08% pays 1 × 100,000 × 0.0008 / 8 = 10 USDso to shorts for that hour.
Funding is applied to your position the next time it is touched, by a trade, a withdrawal or a liquidation, and everything owed since the last time is settled at once. The result is the same as paying every hour.
Both the window and the settlement interval are per-market settings that can change. On testnet every market currently uses an 8-hour window settled hourly.

## https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/developers/perps-testnet (Trade an app wallet with a bot; Common pitfalls)
To have code trade a wallet you use in the app, link your key to it with Link a Bot: the key can then place, cancel and reduce orders for that wallet, and nothing else. The open-source dreamDEX bot kit does this when OWNER_ADDRESS is set to the linked wallet, and the dreamBot Builder sets up a perps bot for you.
The SDK reads the chain over WebSocket. An environment that only proxies HTTP fails on the first chain read with genesis check failed. Give it access to the WebSocket endpoint above.
Paper USDso blocks some markets. Increasing a position in a market outside the credit's allowlist reverts, and getMaxPerpOrderSize reports it as limitedBy: "voucherBlocked".
Each account has a position cap per market, a tenth of the market's open interest cap on most markets. An order that would take the account past it reverts with MaxPositionSizeExceeded() (0x714b36d6); getMaxPerpOrderSize reports the cap as maxPositionSize.
